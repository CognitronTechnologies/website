Global voice rules for a leading agency feel
Lead with the pain or the outcome, not the process.
Use concrete nouns: “reconciliation differences,” “support tickets,” “unmatched transactions,” “handover runbooks,” “audit evidence.”
Prefer active voice and shorter sentences.
Cut repeated phrases like “we agree…,” “you always know…,” “the people who will run it.”
Keep the responsible tone (especially on data, ownership, and AI boundaries) — that’s a strength — but stop sounding like a compliance manual.
Add light proof or specificity wherever possible (even directional: “the kind of portal that cuts status calls,” “the integration that stops month-end fire drills”).
